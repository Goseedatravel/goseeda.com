Mopeka Propane Tank Sensors: Are They Worth It for RVers?

If you've ever wondered how much propane is actually left in your RV tanks, you know the old “pick up the tank and guess” method isn't exactly ideal. Knowing your propane level becomes even more important when you're living and traveling in an RV for extended periods.

We've been using Mopeka propane tank level sensors on our fifth wheel for several months while traveling full-time. Rather than doing a quick unboxing review, we wanted to see how they actually performed after living with them and traveling with them.

What Problem Does the Mopeka Sensor Solve?

A standard removable RV propane cylinder doesn't provide an easy way to look inside and see exactly how much liquid propane remains. That often leaves RV owners estimating their remaining fuel or waiting until a tank runs empty.

Mopeka sensors use ultrasonic technology to measure the propane level from the bottom of the cylinder. The information can then be viewed on a compatible phone or monitoring device.

How the Sensors Install

The sensors mount underneath compatible propane cylinders. Installation is fairly simple, but correct sensor placement and good contact with the bottom of the tank are important for reliable readings.

What We've Learned After Months of Use

Our goal wasn't simply to determine whether the sensors worked on the day we installed them. We wanted to know whether they remained useful through normal RV life and travel.

After several months of use, being able to check propane levels without lifting cylinders or guessing has been genuinely useful for us. It allows us to see when a tank is getting low and plan a refill before running out.

Are Mopeka Propane Sensors Worth It?

For RV owners who use propane regularly, especially while traveling or camping for longer periods, we think having an easier way to monitor propane levels can solve a real problem.
